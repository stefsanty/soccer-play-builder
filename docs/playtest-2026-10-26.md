# Playtest report — Turn Builder (/builder)

- Date: 2026-10-26
- Version: v0.32.1 (commit 6abfaa1)
- Tester: Claude (expert game tester / game designer role)
- Device: mobile viewport 375x812 (iPhone size), Next.js dev server, Chromium
- Objective: command the players to pass the ball around the opponents and score in the enemy goal.

## Result

**Did the tester reach the goal? Yes — but the game does not know it.**

- The tester moved the ball from kick-off to inside the enemy goal in **4 turns** and **10 queued actions** (about 10 minutes, first session, no manual).
- The ball carrier stood **inside the goal, between the posts, with the ball** — and the game silently started Turn 5. No goal, no score, no win screen. See `playtest-assets-2026-10-26/15-ball-inside-goal-no-goal.jpg`.
- Patterns tested: short passes through midfield + run in behind (worked), wing dribble + through ball behind the back line (worked), two-hop cross-field switch + run onto it (worked), dribble into the goal (worked, but not recognized).
- No JavaScript console errors during the whole session.

What works well: the chain model is genuinely good. The hint bar always names the projected holder ("RST will have the ball — tap a teammate to pass, or the pitch to run and meet it"). Passes aim at teammates' *planned* positions. Max-range taps give a clear toast ("Max range — RM runs 21m toward it"). Preview / Play / rewind / "Replan from here" / drag-to-retime all work.

## Top 10 issues (ranked)

### 1. BLOCKER — There is no goal. The objective cannot be completed.
- Steps: dribble or pass the ball to the goal line, then over it. Play the turn.
- What happens: nothing. No shoot action exists. The ball crossed the line and the game moved to the next turn. The carrier can stand in the net next to the keeper.
- Expected: a shoot action, goal detection, feedback (GOAL!), score, and a reset or end state.
- Screenshots: `14-ball-on-goal-line-no-goal.jpg`, `15-ball-inside-goal-no-goal.jpg`.

### 2. MAJOR — All work is lost with one tap. No save, no warning.
- Steps: build 3 turns of play. Tap the back arrow (top-left).
- What happens: instant navigation to the home page. No confirm dialog. Return to /builder: everything is gone. There is also no Save or Share anywhere in /builder — a finished pattern cannot be kept, which defeats the purpose of a pattern builder.
- Screenshots: `22-state-lost-after-back.jpg`.

### 3. MAJOR — Defenders never win the ball. There is no challenge.
- Steps: dribble straight through 4 defenders into the box (Turn 3), or play a cross-field switch past 2 strikers (Turn 2 branch).
- What happens: defenders converge and mark, but never tackle or intercept. A capped 21 m dribble went through six defenders and the keeper untouched.
- Why it matters: as a game, there is no risk, so every plan works and the XCOM-style planning has no tension. As a coaching tool, the "defensive AI" teaches that any pass through traffic succeeds.
- Screenshots: `13-dribble-through-six-defenders.jpg`.

### 4. MAJOR — The pitch resizes while you plan. Taps miss.
- Steps: select a player, queue an action. The queue chips + timeline appear under the pitch, the pitch shrinks, and every player moves up ~20 px. Tap the same player again.
- What happens: the second tap lands on grass (deselects) or on the wrong thing. This caused 3 mis-taps in one session; one mis-tap *selected* a different player instead of queueing a pass, with no error shown.
- Expected: reserve a fixed height for the bottom panel (or overlay it) so pitch coordinates never change during planning.

### 5. MAJOR — A marking defender covers your player. You cannot tap him.
- Steps: end a turn with a defender tight on the ball carrier (happens every turn — the AI marks the holder). Try to select the carrier.
- What happens: the red marker draws on top of the blue one and takes the tap. At the goal mouth the tester had to pixel-hunt a 3 px sliver of blue to select the carrier; one tap on "his" position selected nothing.
- Expected: draw own team (and especially the ball holder) above defenders; prefer blue players in hit-testing; give the holder a larger hit radius.
- Screenshots: `11-holder-hidden-under-defender.jpg`.

### 6. MAJOR — Suggestion flood on entering planning.
- Steps: tap "Start planning".
- What happens: ten dashed arrows with ✓ / × chips appear at once — one per player, including a ~3 m sideways shuffle for the goalkeeper. Chips sit on top of player labels. The measured glyphs are 9×11 px and 5×11 px — far below the 44 px touch minimum. One × chip sat 22 px from the ball holder, a constant mis-tap hazard.
- Expected: show 1–2 suggestions relevant to the queued action, near the ball, with 44 px targets — or put hints behind a toggle.
- Screenshots: `03-suggestion-flood.jpg`, `20-gk-run-suggestion-accepted.jpg`.

### 7. MINOR — Buttons look enabled but silently ignore taps.
- Steps: tap "Start planning" before picking a ball carrier; or tap "Play" with an empty queue.
- What happens: nothing. No disabled style, no shake, no hint flash.
- Expected: clear disabled state, plus a nudge toward the hint text on tap.
- Screenshots: `01-setup-screen.jpg`.

### 8. MINOR — Queue chips overflow off-screen at 375 px.
- Steps: queue 3+ actions.
- What happens: the third chip is cut at the screen edge ("3 LST ru…"); later chips are invisible. No scroll affordance or "+N more" indicator.
- Screenshots: `06-run-queued-chip-truncated.jpg`.

### 9. MINOR — Deleting one action silently deletes its dependents. No undo.
- Steps: queue pass A→B, then pass B→C. Remove the first chip.
- What happens: both vanish with no message. Correct logic, zero feedback — in a 6-action chain a coach loses the whole plan. Also: the trash (clear all) button sits directly beside Play and clears instantly, no confirm.
- Expected: snackbar "Removed 2 dependent actions — Undo".

### 10. MINOR — Pass rules are invisible and inconsistent with run rules.
- Steps: try a direct 70 m switch (rejected: "Too far — a pass can travel up to 63m"); tap beyond run range (accepted, capped: "Max range — runs 21m toward it").
- What happens: a too-far run is capped, a too-far pass is refused. The range disc shows only move range; pass range is never visualized, so the coach discovers the 63 m limit by failing.
- Screenshots: `18-pass-too-far-toast.jpg`, `07-max-range-toast.jpg`.

## Other notes

- **Accessibility**: player circles are raw SVG, not buttons — only 2–7 elements on the page are reachable by screen reader / keyboard. Control buttons do have good aria-labels (Rewind one action, etc.).
- **Setup is one-way**: the "Setup" button disappears once planning starts. A coach cannot reposition players or restart formation without reloading the page.
- **Flash toasts** appear at the top of the pitch, far from where the thumb is; easy to miss (2.2 s).
- **Dev-only**: the Next.js dev-tools badge sits exactly on the Rewind button at 375 px and swallows its taps (it opened the dev menu instead of rewinding). Not a production bug, but it blocks mobile testing on dev builds — move the rewind cluster or the badge.
- **Good**: "Replan from here" works exactly as promised, with a clear flash ("Replanning from turn 2 — later turns discarded"). Drag-to-retime timeline blocks works with a touch-style drag; no numeric start time is shown while dragging (small polish item).

## Quick wins (small effort, big effect)

1. Detect ball over goal line → "GOAL!" banner + confetti + score counter. Even without a shoot action this makes the objective real.
2. `beforeunload` / back-nav confirm + autosave game state to localStorage.
3. Fixed-height (or overlaid) bottom panel so the pitch never resizes mid-plan.
4. Z-order + hit-test priority: own team above defenders, holder on top, bigger hit radius.
5. Cap suggestions to the 2 best; 44 px ✓/× targets; drop GK micro-runs.
6. Real disabled styles on Start planning / Play / Preview.
7. Undo snackbar for chip removal and queue clear.

## Suggested next steps

1. **Make scoring exist** (quick win 1), then add a proper **shoot action** (tap goal = shot, keeper has a save chance based on angle/distance).
2. **Add risk**: interception chance on passes through a defender's reach, tackle chance when dribbling past a defender. Show the risk % on the ghost arrow while planning — that single change turns the builder into a game and teaches real pattern quality.
3. **Save/share from /builder** — reuse the existing /editor save path; the turn history is already a list of steps.
4. **Win condition**: "Score within N turns" gives sessions a shape and an end screen worth sharing.
5. Re-test on a production build on a real phone after fixes 3–5 (layout shift, overlap, suggestion flood) — they are the three biggest sources of mis-taps.
